About Bonsai Rehabilitation Center

Care that's patient, deliberate, and shaped for growth

Like a bonsai tree, every child's progress takes the right conditions, steady attention, and time. We built this center to give families in Peshawar exactly that.

Our Story

Bonsai Rehabilitation Center was founded to close a gap families in Peshawar know well: therapy services scattered across different clinics, different specialists, and different notes that never talk to each other. We brought occupational therapy, physical therapy, speech and language pathology, behavior therapy, sensory integration, special education, and daycare into one center — with one shared plan for every child.

Our Cochlear Implant Montessori Program grew out of the same idea: children with cochlear implants need consistent, specialized auditory-verbal support alongside their everyday learning environment, not instead of it.

Our Approach

  • ✓ Individualized assessment before any plan is written
  • ✓ Therapists collaborate across disciplines on shared goals
  • ✓ Progress tracked and reported back to families, clearly
  • ✓ In-clinic and online therapy, so care fits your routine

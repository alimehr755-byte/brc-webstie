What We Offer

Care built around how your child actually learns and moves

Each therapy plan starts with an assessment, not a template. Our team works together across disciplines so progress in one area supports progress in every other.

Occupational Therapy

Building the fine motor, sensory, and daily-living skills that let a child do more on their own.

Speech & Language Therapy

Helping children find their voice — from first sounds to full conversations.

Physical Therapy

Strength, balance, and movement work designed around each child's stage of development.

Behavior Therapy

Structured, positive strategies that support communication and everyday behavior.

Cochlear Implant Montessori Program

A dedicated auditory-verbal and Montessori-based program for children with cochlear implants.

Prosthetic & Orthotic Care

Custom devices and gait support, assessed and fitted for growing bodies.

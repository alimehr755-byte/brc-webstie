Our Services

Therapy plans built around your child

Every service below is available in-clinic or online, and can be combined into a single, coordinated plan.

Occupational Therapy

Fine motor skills, sensory processing, and independence in everyday activities like dressing, eating, and writing.

Speech & Language Therapy

Articulation, language development, and communication support for children of every age and starting point.

Physical Therapy

Gross motor strength, balance, coordination, and mobility work tailored to each stage of development.

Behavior Therapy

Structured, positive-reinforcement based strategies to support communication, focus, and daily behavior.

Audio-Verbal Therapy

Listening and spoken-language development for children with hearing loss or cochlear implants.

Prosthetic & Orthotic Care

Assessment, fitting, and gait support with devices designed for growing children.

Cochlear Implant Montessori Program

A dedicated Montessori-based learning track built around the auditory and developmental needs of children with cochlear implants.

School Readiness Program

Pre-academic, social, and self-regulation skills to prepare children for a smooth start to formal schooling.

Sensory Integration & Special Education

Individualized education plans and sensory-based interventions for children with diverse learning needs, plus daycare support.

Prefer therapy from home?

All our services are also available as online therapy sessions — just select "Online" when you book.
